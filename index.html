Supporting students in English-medium instruction

Practical guidance and tools for reducing avoidable language-related barriers in teaching, learning and assessment in Vietnamese higher education.

The toolkit supports students from varied English-language backgrounds, with particular consideration of ethnic-minority students. It draws on established practice to support participation and learning while maintaining disciplinary outcomes and academic standards.
