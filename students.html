Practical guidance for students

Use these guides to support your learning through English. Each guide includes practical steps, an example, a checklist, advice on further support and supporting sources.

Start with a difficulty you have experienced, explore the learning areas or search for a topic. Use the guidance alongside your course instructions and assessment requirements. Your teachers and institution also have responsibilities for clear teaching, accessible resources and appropriate support.
