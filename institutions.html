This page outlines the planned content. The guides and tools are being developed and are not yet available.

Planned guidance

EMI program design

Reviewing language demands, making expectations explicit and planning support alongside disciplinary learning.

Identifying support needs

Combining diagnostic information, student perspectives and early learning evidence without assuming needs from ethnicity.

Language-support pathways

Connecting classroom support, academic-language development and additional English-language provision.

Staff development

Supporting educators to practise, collaborate and review inclusive EMI teaching and assessment.

Resource access

Checking access to platforms, materials, equipment and language-learning resources.

Monitoring and improvement

Using participation, student experience, assessment outcomes and support use to inform improvements.
