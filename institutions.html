Practical guidance for institutions

Use these guides to plan, resource and review inclusive EMI provision. They focus on institutional responsibilities for teaching support, language development and access, with particular consideration of ethnic-minority students.

Effective EMI involves teaching practice as well as English-language proficiency. Institutions need to support educators to teach through English and ensure students can access appropriate learning and language support while maintaining disciplinary outcomes and standards.

Build this guidance into existing course and program review, staff development and student-support processes. It complements established institutional procedures and responsibilities.
