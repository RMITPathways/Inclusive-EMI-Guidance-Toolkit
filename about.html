Why this toolkit was developed

This toolkit began with a request to support educators teaching ethnic-minority students through English in Vietnamese higher education.

The needs analysis highlighted a need for practical support with mainstream EMI teaching, learning and assessment. It also highlighted students’ varied educational experiences, languages and access to support. Ethnic-minority students should not be assumed to share the same needs.

Purpose and audiences

The toolkit selects, contextualises and organises established good practice for educators, students and institutions. Its purpose is to reduce avoidable language-related barriers while maintaining disciplinary outcomes and academic standards.

How evidence informs the guidance

The needs analysis informs the areas of need. Published EMI, inclusive-teaching and multilingual-education resources inform the practical guidance. Subject examples, prompts and classroom sequences may be adaptations developed for this toolkit. A source may support a principle or part of a strategy rather than every implementation detail.

The evidence is uneven and context-specific. The toolkit does not claim that all strategies have been tested with ethnic-minority students in Vietnamese higher education. Guidance should be reviewed and adapted to the local context.

Using the toolkit

Choose the section relevant to your role. Identify the task and the difficulty before deciding how to respond. Follow course and institutional requirements and consider whether additional support is needed.
