Inclusive EMI Guidance Toolkit

Practical support for inclusive teaching and learning through English.

Why this toolkit was developed

This toolkit began with a request to support educators teaching ethnic-minority students through English in Vietnamese higher education.

The needs analysis highlighted a need for practical guidance on making mainstream English-medium instruction (EMI) more accessible. It also highlighted the importance of considering students’ varied educational experiences, languages and access to support, rather than assuming that ethnic-minority students share the same needs.

The toolkit therefore supports subject lecturers working with students from varied English-language backgrounds, with particular consideration of ethnic-minority students. Its strategies help students participate in learning and demonstrate their subject knowledge while maintaining the same learning outcomes and academic expectations.

Who it is for

The educator guidance is designed for subject lecturers who teach through English. You do not need to be a language teacher to use it. Practical steps and subject examples explain how to apply each strategy in your teaching.

Educator guidance is the strand currently available. Student and institutional guidance are planned as part of the wider toolkit.

How to use it

Start with a teaching situation or difficulty you have observed. Explore the four groups below, or search for a topic, strategy or subject. Select a difficulty, then choose a strategy to see practical steps, a subject example and supporting guidance.

Choose strategies according to the task and the students you teach. Check what is causing a difficulty before deciding how to respond, and adapt the guidance to your course and institutional requirements.
