Practical guides for students

Choose a guide that matches something you find difficult. Each guide includes practical steps, an example, a checklist, advice on further support and supporting sources. All guides open as PDFs.

You are not expected to resolve every difficulty on your own. Your teachers and institution also have responsibilities for clear teaching, accessible resources and appropriate support.
