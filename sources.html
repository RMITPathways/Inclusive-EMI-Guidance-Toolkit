Acknowledgements and use of sources

This toolkit draws on published EMI, multilingual-education and inclusive-teaching guidance. We acknowledge the authors, institutions and projects listed below.

The toolkit selects and contextualises guidance for educators, students and institutions supporting learning through English in Vietnamese higher education, with particular consideration of ethnic-minority students. Practical sequences, prompts, examples and checklists have been developed or adapted for the toolkit. An external source may support a principle or part of a strategy or guide rather than every implementation detail. The practical guides explain adaptations and limitations.

References identify the original resources; they do not imply endorsement of this toolkit by the authors or institutions. Rights in the original resources remain with their respective rights holders. The TAEC handbook is credited to the TAEC Erasmus+ project (2017–2020), as requested in the handbook.

“n.d.” indicates that a publication date has not been established in this review. Page numbers refer to printed pages in the named resource. A resource's publication or update date is used where established.
