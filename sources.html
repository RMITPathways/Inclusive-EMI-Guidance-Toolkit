Acknowledgements and use of sources

This toolkit draws on published EMI, multilingual-education and inclusive-teaching guidance. We acknowledge the authors, institutions and projects listed below.

The toolkit selects and contextualises guidance for subject lecturers teaching through English in Vietnamese higher education, with particular consideration of ethnic-minority students. Classroom sequences, prompts and subject examples have been developed or adapted for the toolkit. An external source may support a principle or part of a strategy rather than every implementation detail. Review notes identify adaptations and limits where recorded.

References identify the original resources; they do not imply endorsement of this toolkit by the authors or institutions. Rights in the original resources remain with their respective rights holders. The TAEC handbook is credited to the TAEC Erasmus+ project (2017–2020), as requested in the handbook.

Developing draft: references and source-to-strategy mappings require final editorial and local review. “n.d.” indicates that a publication date has not been established in this review. Page numbers refer to printed pages in the named resource. A resource's publication or update date is used where established.
